# Privacy Policy

nia does not collect or transmit personal data. Documents and settings stay on your computer.

nia writes brief operational logs to the process's standard error stream, such as document open and save events, theme changes, and failures. Logs do not include document text, search queries, or file paths. nia does not transmit logs. If you launch nia from a terminal or another program that records standard error, that program may retain the logs.

For crash recovery, nia keeps temporary local copies of unsaved document text in its application support directory. It removes a draft after that document is saved or its clean tab is closed. Recovered drafts appear as unsaved tabs when nia starts again. The recent-files list stores local file paths in the same application support directory.
